The pathway

A clear road from first words to B2

Five programmes, each one a step on the CEFR scale. Learners move up when they are ready, not when the calendar says so.

  1. Pre-A1

    Ages 5–7

    Seedling

    First words, songs and stories. Listening and speaking come before reading.

    Practice tasks in the style of Pre A1 Starters

  2. A1

    Ages 7–9

    Sapling

    Short sentences about home, school and friends, with first reading and writing routines.

    Practice tasks in the style of A1 Movers

  3. A2

    Ages 9–11

    Branch

    Longer conversations, short texts and first paragraphs of connected writing.

    Practice tasks in the style of A2 Flyers and A2 Key

  4. B1

    Ages 11–13

    Canopy

    Giving opinions, reading longer texts and writing emails and short stories.

    Practice tasks in the style of B1 Preliminary

  5. B2

    Ages 13–15

    Crown

    Discussion, essays and reports, with reading practice that also prepares for later study.

    Practice tasks in the style of B2 First, with IELTS-style reading practice

Exam names describe the style of practice tasks only. This academy is not affiliated with or endorsed by Cambridge University Press & Assessment or the IELTS owners, and nothing here promises exam results.

How we teach

Three habits in every class

  • 01

    Small groups

    Few enough learners that every child speaks in every class. Group size is stated for each programme.

  • 02

    All four skills

    Listening, speaking, reading and writing every week, with the balance shifting as the level rises.

  • 03

    Written feedback

    A short report each term in plain words, with the next step for the learner and for home.

Progress, in plain words

A progress report parents can actually read

Each term every learner gets a short written report. It describes what they can do now and what to practise next, without scores or rankings.

  • Four skills described in everyday language
  • A teacher comment with one clear next step
  • A recommendation for the next programme when the time comes

Questions parents ask

Does my child have to take the placement visit?

We recommend it for every new learner, because the right level matters more than age. The visit is short and relaxed, and parents get written feedback.

What if my child has never studied English?

That is fine. Younger beginners start in Seedling, and older beginners are placed in the level where they can follow the class and still feel challenged.

Do you prepare learners for Cambridge or IELTS exams?

Some classes use practice tasks in the style of those exams, which helps learners know the format. We are not affiliated with the exam owners and we do not promise results.

Can my child move up in the middle of a term?

Sometimes. If the teacher and the academic team agree the class is too easy, we will suggest a move and explain the fee difference.
