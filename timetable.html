About the timetable

Can a learner attend two programmes?

Usually not at the same time. We place each learner in the one level that fits best, and add extra practice only after a teacher recommends it.

What if my chosen slot is full?

We keep a waiting list for each slot and suggest the nearest alternative.

Are classes held on public holidays?

No. The academy publishes its term dates and holiday closures before each term starts.
